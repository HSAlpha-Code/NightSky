# NightSky: publisher and market legal notes

Prepared 17 November 2026 for Hemant Sharma, an individual publisher in India. Scope: a free astronomy app, no accounts, advertising, analytics, paid features or child-directed positioning; intended distribution in supported countries excluding restricted destinations. This is release preparation, not a determination that every country's law has been satisfied. Nothing has been submitted to Google or published by this review.

## Decisions and evidence still needed before release

- Confirm the actual publisher name and verified Play account identity match Hemant Sharma; supply a monitored public support/privacy email. Do not put a private home address or phone number into public documents without checking the relevant Play disclosure requirements and the intended contact details.
- Record the exact country/territory list selected in Play Console. “Worldwide except sanctioned locations” is an intent, not an auditable list. Use Play's current availability and applicable restrictions; do not promise availability everywhere. Recheck this list before submission and before adding markets.
- Determine trader/non-trader status from the actual activity, including whether development is a business or profession and whether regular monetization is intended. A free initial release does not by itself prove non-trader status. See the Google declaration dates below.
- Confirm the actual Vercel project, hosting/processing regions, provider contractual terms, log contents and retention, backups, access controls and deletion options. Hosting-provider processing is still relevant when the application does not deliberately save uploads. No production hosting dashboard or contracts were inspected for this note.
- Document photo, GPS/altitude, timestamp, orientation, device/IP/request-log handling separately from the local image cache. Verify whether request bodies, exception reporting, temporary files and reverse-proxy logs preserve any user data. Avoid “no data collected,” “never stored,” or “everything stays on your phone” claims for camera uploads.
- Select and document the actual age audience in Play Console and the store listing. “Not child-directed” is a product intention; it does not establish that no children can use the app or remove country-specific children's privacy duties if actual use changes.
- Name the person handling privacy requests, how to identify any records that actually exist without gathering unnecessary new identity documents, the response process and the incident-response contact. For data never retained, explain that accurately rather than promising deletion of nonexistent records.
- Obtain a focused jurisdiction review for the final market list, particularly EEA/UK targeting and any local representation or cross-border-transfer requirements. No-account/free status is not a global privacy-law exemption.

## India: what is in force and what is upcoming

The DPDP Act and Rules use phased commencement. The November 2025 commencement notification brings definitions, Board/institutional provisions and specified other sections into force first; the consent-manager framework follows after one year; the principal data-fiduciary duties, data-principal rights and related enforcement provisions follow after eighteen months. The Rules have a matching staged schedule. The Gazette notifications are dated 13 November 2025; their electronic publication identifiers bear 14 November 2025. [Act commencement notification, G.S.R. 843(E)](https://meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf), [final Rules, G.S.R. 846(E), rule 1](https://meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).

For explicit calendar dates, MeitY's PIB release of **14 November 2026** confirms the consent-manager registration framework took effect on **13 November 2026**, and the principal rights, fiduciary obligations and inquiry/penalty provisions are scheduled for **13 May 2027**. Thus this note does not present the main DPDP processing duties as already fully in force on 17 November 2026. NightSky requesting consent for its own functionality does not by itself make Hemant a registered Consent Manager. [PIB implementation update](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2336151).

Prepare clear itemized data/purpose notices, withdrawal and rights-request channels, proportionate safeguards, deletion schedules and breach procedures now. Recheck the framework before May 2027 and assess continuing Indian IT/privacy/security requirements during the transition. Do not infer that delayed DPDP commencement means no current privacy obligations: Google Play's policies already apply, and other applicable law needs separate assessment.

## Google Play: current policy and announced declaration dates

All apps need a public, accessible, non-geofenced, non-PDF privacy policy URL and a policy link/text within the app. Identify the publisher/contact, accessed and transmitted data, purposes, recipients, security and retention/deletion. The policy must match the shipped application and Data Safety form. No accounts means the account-deletion requirement is not triggered merely by installing this version; it does not remove retention/deletion disclosures or rights under applicable law. [User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en).

Uploads for real-time processing must still be assessed in the Data Safety form. Google's ephemeral category requires memory-only processing for no longer than necessary for the real-time request; qualifying data may be omitted from the public label but must be included in form responses. On-device-only processing has a different treatment. A qualifying processor/service-provider transfer may be excluded from the form's “sharing” classification without eliminating privacy disclosure duties. Final answers require verified production behavior. [Data Safety instructions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en).

Google's current trader-status guidance says **new developer accounts declare immediately**, and **existing accounts must declare by 1 February 2027**. The guidance concerns EEA/UK trader classification, but requires the declaration globally. The page describes new marketplace disclosures beginning **1 March 2027**. Traders are asked for a Terms of Service URL; personal-account traders must supply a public contact phone number and their address is displayed. Google lists commercial purpose, business setup and intended remuneration among the factors. The classification remains the publisher's legal responsibility; this review has not selected or submitted a declaration. [Trader status declaration](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17594752?hl=en).

## EEA/UK and worldwide distribution

The European Commission explains that GDPR can apply to an organisation outside the EU offering services to people in the EU **even when the service is free**. Location and IP information can be personal data. Selecting EU markets therefore warrants a real territorial-scope assessment, rather than assuming that India-based hosting or a hobby/free app avoids the issue. [Commission GDPR application guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/application-gdpr_en).

Where GDPR applies, identify the lawful basis for each purpose, provide clear information at collection, document rights/contact and retention, use appropriate security and provider processing contracts, and plan breach response. Assess whether EU representation and international-transfer safeguards are required for the actual processing arrangement. Assess UK requirements separately; an EU-focused review does not establish UK compliance. This note does not decide representation/transfer exemptions or claim a mandatory DPO for this small app. [Commission application and processor guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/application-gdpr_en), [Commission obligations guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/obligations_en).

## Content for the final privacy notice

The final notice should cover Hemant Sharma and contact details; effective date; camera uploads versus local Sky View processing; location, timestamp and orientation; technical connection/log data; purposes and applicable lawful bases; hosting and other recipient roles; international processing and applicable safeguards; verified retention periods/criteria for each data flow; deletion and other rights/contact procedures; permission controls/manual-location alternatives; current absence of accounts, ads and analytics; intended audience; policy changes; and any relevant supervisory complaint rights. Do not invent retention periods, provider guarantees or legal bases to fill template gaps. An Android permission prompt alone does not explain unexpected server uploads; show an appropriate in-app explanation and obtain consent when Play policy requires it.

## Later support payments

This version has no paid features. Before adding a donation/tip button or linking a support-payment page from the app, review the exact flow, recipient, benefits, regions and current Play rules. Google's guidance permits a direct user-to-creator tip without Play billing when **100% goes to the creator** and it grants no digital content/services, including badges. A paid unlock, badge or other benefit changes that analysis. An ordinary individual publisher should not describe support payments as tax-exempt charitable donations without a valid basis. Do not assume that moving checkout to a website makes an in-app link unrestricted; regional external-payment programs have conditions. [Google payment-policy explanation](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10281818?hl=en).
